# TWF-231: Northgate fund admin sync

**Type:** Epic · **Priority:** High · **Reporter:** Leo Varga · **Due:** 2026-12-18

## Description

Keep investor records in sync between the platform and Northgate Fund Services (fictional): names, entities, addresses, commitments, bank details.

- Changes in Northgate show up in the platform within one business day
- Sync failures alert the ops team
- Ops can see a log of what changed
- Bank detail changes require human approval before applying
