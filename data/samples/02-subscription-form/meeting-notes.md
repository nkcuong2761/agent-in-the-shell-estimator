# Discovery call: online subscription form

Attendees: Priya Natarajan (COO), Marcus Bell (fund counsel, external), Oakmere ops lead, delivery lead

## What we heard

- The paper form has ~140 fields. Ops estimates 30% of fields are conditional.
- Marcus (counsel) needs to review every disclosure and consent sentence before launch. He said "two weeks, maybe more around the holidays".
- There are separate paper versions for individuals and for entities. There may also be a trust version: Priya to check. Unclear whether we build one form with branches or several forms.
- Signing: today investors sign by hand on the last page. Priya: "Let's just get the form online first, we can figure out signatures later." Marcus looked unconvinced.
- Records: ops keeps investor records in "the investor database". We didn't establish whether it has an API or whether we'd write to it some other way.
- Investors also send supporting documents (ID, W-9/W-8). No one said whether those come through the new form.
- Accessibility: Priya mentioned some older investors struggle with web forms. No specific standard requested.
- Save and come back later: "definitely needed, it's a long form".

## Open

- Number of form variants (individual / entity / trust?).
- How signatures will work at launch.
- How data gets into the investor database.
